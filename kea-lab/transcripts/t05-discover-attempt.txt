Result: the packet is real but undeliverable. The sandbox refuses
every UDP send (see t02), so no DISCOVER can reach the server and
no OFFER could come back. The lease dance itself is therefore
documented-only in this guide; the lease database is exercised
directly through the control channel instead (t07, t08).
